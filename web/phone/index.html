Android
  1. Open Settings → About phone and tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Open Developer options and turn on USB debugging.
  3. Plug the phone into your PC with a USB cable.
  4. On the PC, open SCraMic Share and click Wired.
  5. Tap Allow on this phone. SCraMic Share opens here by itself.
iPhone

We recommend Wireless on iPhone. It is much more stable than Wired.

  1. Open Settings → Personal Hotspot and turn on Allow Others to Join.
  2. Plug the iPhone into your PC with the cable. Tap Trust if asked.
  3. On the PC, open SCraMic Share, click Wired, and scan the iPhone QR code.

Personal Hotspot needs Cellular Data turned on. SCraMic Share itself uses no data over the cable. If your carrier blocks the hotspot, use Wireless instead.
